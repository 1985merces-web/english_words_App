Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-06

This Privacy Policy explains what data the App processes, why it is processed, and what choices you have.

Summary

  • The App works offline using built-in word lists.
  • The App stores your settings and custom lists locally on your device.
  • The App does not collect or sell personal data.

Data the App processes

  • App settings (e.g., selected Study/Native language, app preferences). Stored locally.
  • Custom lists (words you enter). Stored locally.
  • Playback actions (text-to-speech). Processed on-device via your operating system's TTS engine.

How we use data

  • To provide core learning functionality (show cards, lists, and progress in the session).
  • To remember your preferences and custom lists (stored on-device).

Data storage and retention

  • On-device storage: The App stores settings and custom lists on your device.
  • Retention: Data remains until you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.

Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience educational tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Your choices

  • You can delete custom lists and/or uninstall the App to remove locally stored data.
