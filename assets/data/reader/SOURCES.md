# Reader Text Sources

These are unabridged English narrative chapters, without illustrations,
translations, publisher front matter, or Gutenberg header/footer/license.
Text is divided into chapters and wrapped lines are reflowed into paragraphs.
The included original texts have not been graded or adapted to CEFR levels.

`tools/scripts/prepare_reader_books.js` generates the JSON assets from the
five corresponding UTF-8 plain-text editions. It verifies chapter counts and
their Roman numeral order before writing. The source hashes above identify the
precise input files downloaded on 2026-10-19.
